# T06: Plain-language reasons, name every duplicated product

Acceptance (owner: Marketing): Marketing's examples in `acceptance/marketing/EXAMPLES.md` (section T06) and the
protected tests in `acceptance/marketing/t06.test.mjs` (tests `T06 …`). Do not change them.

Intent (Marketing, verbatim): "A reviewer should understand the reason without knowing any system terms, and every
duplicated product should be named."

Builds on T05 (`Invoice.prototype.reviewSummary`). Change only the human-facing text:
- The summary `reason` and every flag `reason` contain no system terms: no flag codes (`empty`, `duplicate_line`,
  `over_threshold`) and no underscore/snake_case identifiers. Flag `code` fields stay as they are (they are for machines).
- The summary `reason` says what is wrong in plain words: a duplicate is described as a duplicate / appearing more than
  once (and names the product), a large amount refers to the threshold or limit, an empty invoice says it has no lines.
- Every duplicated SKU is named, in the flag reason(s) and in the summary `reason` (e.g. A×3 and B×2 name both A and B).
  One `duplicate_line` flag per SKU or one flag listing all of them are both fine. A SKU that appears once is not reported.
- The summary `reason` is still one sentence: a single line, ending with `.`, at most 200 characters, also with
  three duplicated products.
- Everything else in T05 is unchanged (keys, totals, codes, duplicate rules, threshold rules, TypeError on JS numbers).
  This supersedes one T05 clause: the summary `reason` no longer contains flag codes (T05's test was amended to match).
Add tests.

Human judgement (not tested; Marketing reads real output): whether the sentence is natural and immediately clear,
tone, word order, whether counts ("three times") are given, and whether the approve sentence is reassuring enough.
