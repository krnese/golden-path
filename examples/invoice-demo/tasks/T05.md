# T05: Review summary a human can approve at a glance

Acceptance: Marketing's examples in `acceptance/EXAMPLES.md` (section T05) and the protected tests in
`acceptance/test_acceptance.py` (class `T05`). Do not change them.

Add `Invoice.review_summary(threshold="50000.00")` returning a dict with **exactly** these keys:
`lines` (int, number of lines), `net`, `vat`, `gross` (Decimal, equal to `net_total()`,
`vat_total()`, `gross_total()`), `flags` (list of `{"code": str, "reason": str}`),
`recommendation` (`"approve"` or `"review"`) and `reason` (str).

Flags, each with a plain-language `reason` that names what is wrong:
- `empty`: the invoice has no lines.
- `duplicate_line`: two or more lines have the same `sku`, `unit_price`, `quantity` and `vat_class`;
  the reason names the SKU.
- `over_threshold`: `gross` is strictly greater than `threshold` (str or Decimal; floats rejected
  like `to_money`); the reason states the threshold.

`recommendation` is `"approve"` if and only if `flags` is empty, otherwise `"review"`. `reason` is one
sentence: a single line, ending with `.`, at most 200 characters; when reviewing it contains every
flag's code. Do not change existing behaviour. Add tests.
