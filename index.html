THE ENGINEERING AGREEMENT

Keep what matters clear, from intent to evidence.

Golden Path is a lightweight engineering method with optional checks. Start with a developer change, a business outcome, an analytical task or an investigation.

  1. 01

    Agree what matters

    Make the outcome, authoritative context, permitted actions and decision owner clear. Agree what evidence will support a judgement; surface uncertainty that could change the next step.

  2. 02

    Work within the agreement

    The agent investigates, builds and tests inside those boundaries. If new information changes the target or permitted effects, return to the appropriate owner.

  3. 03

    Return evidence for a decision

    Show actual results against agreed examples, the checked revision and remaining gaps. Accountable owners judge whether the result meets the need.

This engineering contract lives in your existing tasks, policies, approved examples, tests and review records. It can start with a bounded investigation. The person asking may not be authorised to approve the rules or effects. Merge and deployment still need their own permissions.

02 / START WITH YOUR NEXT TASK

You bring the idea.
Copilot does the legwork.

Open your project in VS Code. Use your usual Copilot agent. You don't need a formal specification or a new agent to pick.

What you install: a small repository kit with a skill, optional Copilot hooks, and a CI workflow template.

A shared engineering agreement: the domain expert explains the need; the appropriate owner approves the rules and permitted effects. An engineer helps turn examples into tests, configures the controls, and maintains them.

  1. 01

    Say what you want

    “Make our invoice screen easier to understand. Keep the calculations unchanged.”

  2. 02

    Agree a few examples

    “If a product appears twice, flag it for review and name the product.”

  3. 03

    Look at the result

    “Show me the real output for our examples, and tell me what's still uncertain.”

Already have tests and branch protection?

Keep them. The engineering agreement lives in your existing records. In a configured checked setup, protected acceptance, bounded local feedback, required CI and human review help preserve it. Passing examples provide evidence for the owner's judgement; they do not prove the whole business outcome.

Prefer the terminal? Installation and checked setup

Needs Node.js 22+ and git. From a clone of Golden Path, the simple starting option is:

node plugin/install.mjs --target "<your-repo>" --level 1

It adds instructions, not automatic checking or enforcement. For owner-controlled acceptance, agree the tests and commands with your team, then use:

From a clone of the repository
git clone https://github.com/krnese/golden-path.git
cd golden-path
node plugin/install.mjs --target "<your-repo>" --level 2 --check "<fast-check-command>" --acceptance-path "acceptance/**" --acceptance-owner "<named-owner>" --acceptance-command "<acceptance-test-command>" --with-ci

Replace the placeholders with your repository, existing test commands and named acceptance owner. Approve the tests before installing. The installer preserves existing configuration.

Then configure CODEOWNERS, required CI and Code Owner review, and verify refusal plus legitimate owner revisions. Follow the adoption checklist →

03 / INTERFACE IS NOT AUTHORITY

Assist is not decide.

Use the agent you already have. Keep the definition of success outside its control.

What assists development and what decides executable acceptance
AssistDecide
CopilotProposes and implements code.Owner-approved acceptance on protected mainThe executable examples the implementation must meet.
Hooks and the local gateCatch common edits and return bounded feedback.Required CI on the PRChecks the implementation and integrity against the PR base.
Skills and Copilot reviewGuide the work. Review is advisory.Branch protection and owner reviewGovern merge and approved changes to acceptance.

These are controls you configure, not permissions Golden Path grants. The method can travel with the work; enforcement depends on the host and configured controls. The installer does not enable GitHub branch protection.

04 / THE HONEST LIMITS

What it doesn't do.

  • Replace engineering judgment

    Tests prove their examples, not every business outcome. Owners still inspect evidence and engineers still review changes.

  • Make local hooks tamper-proof

    Protection is heuristic. Unreadable evidence is UNVERIFIED, not a pass. Retry exhaustion or a host timeout can allow stopping without a pass. Required CI remains essential.

  • Turn Copilot review into approval

    Review comments are advice. Your configured review and merge rules remain the authority.

  • Promise a productivity boost

    The mechanism is tested and the workflow demonstrated. Incremental benefit over competent engineering is still unproved.

Golden Path

Agree what matters. Implement within the agreement. Return evidence for judgement.
